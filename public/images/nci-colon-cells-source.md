# Homepage microscopy image

Human Colon Cancer Cells, NCI Center for Cancer Research, from the NCI Cancer Close Up 2015 collection.

Source: https://www.flickr.com/photos/nihgov/42301616611
Original file: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Human_Colon_Cancer_Cells_(42301616611).jpg
Rights: Public domain, NIH Image Gallery. NCI reuse policy: https://www.cancer.gov/policies/copyright-reuse

The image shows red-stained nuclei and green E-cadherin staining. It illustrates cancer cell biology; it is not an image of IP6-treated cells or evidence of an IP6 treatment effect.

The original 3000 x 3000 image is served as 960 and 1920 pixel WebP versions. Responsive crops and a dark overlay maintain readable homepage text. Image credit and a source link appear in the hero.

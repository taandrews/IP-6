# Shared IP6 branding

Brand alignment completed September 10, 2026. The public storefront returned Shopify's password screen, so its rendered brand could not be verified anonymously. The source for this update is the local IP6 Shopify theme.

- Primary navy: #0B1F3A
- Body text: #1A2A40
- Gold accents: #C5A253 and #A8842F
- White background: #FFFFFF
- Light surface: #F4F7FA
- Warm ivory: #F7F5F0
- Muted text: #5B6470

Colors come from snippets/ip6-head.liquid and config/settings_data.json in the Shopify project. The logo file is copied unchanged from the source image used by ip6-pw/logo.mjs to upload ip6-logo2.png, which the Shopify settings select.

The research property retains its own name, research content, and navigation. No commerce links or storefront account functions were added.
